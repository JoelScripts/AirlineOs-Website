The universal engine

WORKS WITH YOUR AIRLINE

No airline content packs. Ever. Identity flows through a resolution chain — from you, to your flight plan, to the sim itself:

1  Manual override› 2  SimBrief flight plan› 3  Live livery from the sim› 4  Generic fallback
  • Reads your livery's airline name, registration and flight number live via SimConnect
  • Imports your latest SimBrief OFP — airline code, callsign, route, fuel and weather
  • Fills the sim's ATC identity from your flight plan, so native ATC calls you by your callsign
  • If you configure nothing at all, it still works — zero setup required
